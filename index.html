Why work at Edbridge

Lesson for evvery learner

Our apps follow WCAG 2.2 so learners who use screen readers, keyboards or small phones can study too. You will help keep it that way.

From Vietnamese to Spanish, every course is written with native-speaker teachers. Staff get free lessons in any of them.

Flexible, hybrid work

Two studio days a week in Melbourne, the rest from wherever you learn best, plus a yearly learning budget.
