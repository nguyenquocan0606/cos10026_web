Why work at Edbridge

Lessons for every learner

Our apps follow WCAG 2.2 so learners who use screen readers, keyboards or small phones can study too. You will help keep it that way.

12 languages and counting

From Vietnamese to Spanish, every course is written with native-speaker teachers. Staff get free lessons in any of them.

Flexible, hybrid work

Two studio days a week in Melbourne, the rest from wherever you learn best, plus a yearly learning budget.

How we hire

Our process is short and transparent. Tell us in your application if you need any adjustments at any stage.

Edbridge hiring process, step by step
Stage Step What happens Typical time
Apply Online form Choose a job reference number and tell us about your skills. 10 minutes
Interviews Team chat A relaxed video call with two people from the team you would join. 30 minutes
Practical task A small, paid take-home task based on a real lesson page. 2 hours
Offer Decision We call you with a decision and feedback, whatever the outcome. Within 1 week
