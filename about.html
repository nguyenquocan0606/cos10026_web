Respect and inclusion in education

We recognise Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples as the First Peoples of Australia and respect their continuing connections to Country, cultures and communities.

As a student team in Hanoi developing an education careers website, we want Edbridge to reflect respect for the people and communities that education serves. We welcome Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander applicants and value the knowledge and perspectives they bring to education.

For this project, our commitment means using respectful language, making career information accessible, and avoiding stereotypes in how we describe people and roles. Edbridge is a student project; this statement expresses our team's values and does not claim a community partnership.
